## Critical Performance Issues

### Q4 shortfall is large, concentrated, and worsens in December

**FACT** → The 216 native-grain Q4 forecast/target combinations include
162 negative gaps totaling **$19.760B** and 54 positive gaps totaling
**$17.423B**; the positive intersections offset, but do not erase, the
resulting **-$2.337B net gap**.

**FINDING** → The five largest Region × Channel × Category shortfall
intersections account for **28.80%** of gross negative shortfall:

| Intersection | Q4 negative gap | Share of gross shortfall |
|---|---:|---:|
| South × GT × Beverages | -$1.339B | 6.78% |
| South × GT × Snacks | -$1.233B | 6.24% |
| North × GT × Beverages | -$1.141B | 5.77% |
| North × GT × Snacks | -$0.999B | 5.06% |
| South × MT × Beverages | -$0.978B | 4.95% |

**BUSINESS IMPACT / RISK** → December has the largest projected monthly gap
(-$1.306B; 92.23% achievement). The forecast identifies where review is
commercially relevant; it does not establish causes or recoverable revenue.

**RECOMMENDED FOCUS** → Validate local drivers, accountable owners, and
assumptions first in the largest intersections, while tracking the December
gap separately from October and November.

### Core customers with validated High decline risk merit retention review

**FACT** → The Build 1 action cohort contains **617 Core / Maintain customers
with a validated High decline-risk tier**, **$20.177B** in trailing-12-month
(T12M) sales, and **$7.397B** in T12M gross margin. Their Jul–Sep sales were
**$6.716B**, of which **$1.919B** mapped to current customer
Region × Channel × Category intersections with a negative Q4 gap.

**FINDING** → This is a sizeable existing customer base with a validated
relative decline signal and a measurable intersection with Q4 gap areas.

**BUSINESS IMPACT / RISK** → T12M exposure is business scale, not expected
loss. The decline score is not probability-calibrated; the mapped recent sales
are not sales at risk or recoverable revenue.

**RECOMMENDED FOCUS** → Have account teams review observed trends and account
context for the highest-priority Core / Maintain customers. Do not use
provisional inactivity as an independent trigger.

### Potential customers represent a base to validate, not forecast uplift

**FACT** → **153 Potential customers** meet the existing Momentum ≥70,
Engagement ≥50 qualification and have positive T12M sales. Together they have
**$3.764B** T12M sales and **$1.367B** T12M gross margin. Their Jul–Sep sales
were **$1.416B**, of which **$0.465B** mapped to negative-gap intersections.

**FINDING** → The segment is a relative commercial cohort with engagement
and momentum signals; no incremental-sales estimate is produced.

**BUSINESS IMPACT / RISK** → T12M exposure is the existing business base, not
an opportunity forecast. Recent sales in gap areas are not assumed
recoverable.

**RECOMMENDED FOCUS** → Validate observed momentum, customer interest, and
capacity with the account team before recording any opportunity.

## Gaps / Risks

### Inventory flags identify execution reviews, not future stockouts

**FACT** → The Build 1 queue contains **741** demand-supported inventory
positions in the September 30 snapshot: **525 High** and **216 Medium**
coverage-pressure positions; **602** also have a current On Hand below
Reorder Point exception. These positions carry **148,691 units** of observed
trailing-three-complete-month demand.

**FINDING** → Current reorder exceptions and analytical coverage-pressure
signals are present in categories with Q4 negative shortfall. Inventory is
linked to Q4 relevance at **Category only**; distributor-to-region/channel
mapping is unsafe.

**BUSINESS IMPACT / RISK** → Reorder exception is a current condition.
Coverage pressure is a screening signal, not a confirmed stockout or a
lead-time-calibrated prediction. In-transit quantity is not physically
available stock.

**RECOMMENDED FOCUS** → Review current On Hand, Reorder Point, recent demand,
and inbound status under normal replenishment policy. Do not infer a
stockout, lost sales, or exact order quantity.
